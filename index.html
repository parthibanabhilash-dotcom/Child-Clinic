A familiar toy, blanket, or favourite book can make a new place feel a little more like home.

No question is too small.

That thing you’ve been wondering about? Write it down. This is a good place to ask.

Come just as you are.

A sleepy baby. A shy toddler. A parent with a long list. You’re all welcome here.

What should we bring to our first visit?+

Bring your child’s previous health records, vaccination record, a list of any medicines, and your questions. A favourite toy or comfort item is welcome, too.

Can I book a visit for a newborn?+

Yes. Select “Newborn & baby care” in the form to prepare a visit request. Availability would need to be confirmed by the clinic.

What if my child feels nervous about the doctor?+

Let us know what helps your child feel comfortable. Familiar toys, simple explanations, and time for questions can help make the visit easier.

How do appointment requests work?+

This website is a design preview. The form prepares a request locally; it does not send information or book an appointment.
